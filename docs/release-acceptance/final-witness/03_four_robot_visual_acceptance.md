## 2. Nine-Phase Locomotion & Handoff Proof

For each robot, the physical and spatial animation system strictly models 9 operational phases:

1. **Appearance & Idle:** Character executes sinusoidal hovering physics with ambient glow.
2. **Target Approach:** Trajectory vector computed using cubic Bézier easing.
3. **Pickup Animation:** Articulated arms extend to capture the glowing event packet.
4. **Packet Attachment:** Packet coordinates bind to character hand nodes during transit.
5. **Whole-Body Physical Movement:** Acceleration and bank-angle tilting proportional to velocity vector.
6. **Path Traversal:** Continuous movement along hardware-accelerated SVG energy conduits without discrete coordinate jumping.
7. **Arrival & Deceleration:** Eased landing at destination handoff boundary.
8. **Confirmed Delivery:** Transmission of packet to receiving agent upon schema validation.
9. **Pause, Resume & Reset:** Spacebar cleanly pauses and resumes locomotion loops; Esc resets positions to origin stations.
