### 1. Methodology & Distinction from Static Seeded Frames

In Milestone 51, it was noted that static frames generated by forcing query parameters (e.g. `?progress=0.45`) test geometry but do not prove continuous physical movement.

To establish genuine temporal movement:
1. Firefox 140.16.0esr was launched via Marionette protocol on port 2828.
2. The relay was started via natural trigger without forced progress overrides.
3. The browser DOM and `window.__HSX_RELAY_STATE__` were sampled at ~350ms intervals over an uninterrupted 14.5-second sequence.
4. Robot coordinates, progress percentages, packet clamping states, and DOM text were recorded synchronously.
